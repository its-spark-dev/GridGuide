GridGuide · Generic outage preparedness
Content reviewed November 3, 2026. This saved guidance is not live outage awareness.

Before an outage:
- Keep battery-powered lighting and a battery or crank radio accessible.
- Keep non-perishable food, drinking water, and spare batteries ready.
- Keep a paper contact list and arrange how your household will stay in touch.

During an outage:
- Use a flashlight instead of candles.
- Keep refrigerator and freezer doors closed as much as possible.
- Keep generators outdoors, away from windows. Never use an outdoor stove indoors.
- Stay away from fallen power lines and objects touching them. Contact local emergency services about immediate hazards.
- Follow local official alerts when you can reconnect. This kit has no live outage information.

These general reminders are not personalized medical, evacuation or electrical-work instructions.
Source: American Red Cross, Power Outage Safety
https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/types-of-emergencies/power-outage.html

GridGuide does not send alerts or confirm utility restoration. Printing or downloading this generic kit does not include your scenario, declarations or personal information.
