# Sources, rights and attribution

This is a documented evidence review, not a grant of rights in third-party materials.
Raw archives, workbooks, downloaded pages and documents remain in ignored local cache.
No original external data rows or copied paper figures are included in this PR.

## PNNL v2: artifact-specific catalog grant established

Buxin She, Veronica Adetola and Ji Young Yun. *Event-correlated Outage Dataset in America*.
Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, Outage Dataset v2, OEDI submission 6458.
[Official catalog](https://data.openei.org/submissions/6458) ·
[DOE catalog mirror](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/event-correlated-outage-dataset-in-america) ·
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The DOE catalog's structured dataset metadata explicitly includes the chosen
`https://data.openei.org/files/6458/Outage_Dataset_R1.zip` distribution and the CC BY 4.0
license URI. This binds the reviewed catalog grant to the specific resource, rather than
assuming that all DOE-hosted material has identical rights. The archive contains the same
disclaimer as the separately linked document; it contains no conflicting embedded license.
Acquisition receipts hash both catalog pages, the archive, guide and disclaimer.

CC BY requires attribution, a license link and identification of changes. The audit JSON,
tables and original GridGuide figures are adaptations/aggregate analyses of those source
values, with that attribution retained; use **CC BY 4.0 for those source-derived research
artifacts**, without implying that the repository MIT grant relicenses external material.
Original audit software remains under the repository MIT license.

Changes: schema validation, counts, duplicate diagnostics, duration summaries/histograms,
and visualization. No source values were corrected. PNNL/Battelle/DOE provide no warranty
or endorsement; the disclaimer does not supply evidence of accuracy or third-party rights.
PNNL is operated by Battelle for DOE under contract DE-AC05-76RL01830. The catalog credits
the PNNL LDRD Resilience Through Data-Driven, Intelligently Designed Control initiative.

Inherited sources are named rather than erased: **EAGLE-I**, **DOE-417**, and Census
**CO-EST2024-POP**, as stated by the PNNL guide. The linked EAGLE-I record currently resolves
to [Brelsford et al., version 4](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24237376.v4), explicitly
CC BY 4.0. Its full author list and version are in the downloaded metadata. That current
2014–2025 record is **not asserted to be the exact upstream revision used to build PNNL's
2014–2023 package**. DOE-417 and Census raw inputs were not downloaded or republished;
no blanket inherited redistribution permission or complete upstream reproducibility is
claimed. Source attribution remains linked through the PNNL guide and catalog.

## Purdue: public access is not an explicit workbook license

Sayanti Mukherjee, Roshanak Nateghi and Makarand Hastak. *Data on major power outage events
in the continental U.S.* Data in Brief 19 (2018), 2079–2083.
[Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2018.06.067) ·
[Official workbook landing page](https://engineering.purdue.edu/LASCI/research-data/outages).

The article expressly carries CC BY 4.0 and links the dataset. The workbook and landing
page do not expressly state that grant for the workbook; the landing page has a general
Purdue copyright footer. No license keyword was found in the workbook's shared strings,
and no embedded license file was found. The article's grant may have been intended to
cover the accompanying data, but this review does not turn that interpretation into
confirmed artifact-specific permission. **Unresolved**, not “prohibited.”

Only factual audit counts, schema names and acquisition identity are published. The workbook
is not redistributed, no learned artifact is produced from it, and the user-requested rights
gate remains closed pending explicit confirmation. No author was contacted automatically.

the dataset does not erase separately identified third-party rights.

## Reproducibility boundary

Static data/download pins are exact. Catalog metadata may change: saved receipts identify
the reviewed snapshots; a later acquisition can have a different metadata hash and requires
comparison rather than overwriting this evidence. Mutable source URLs are not archival
guarantees. Checksums show byte identity, not publisher authenticity or label truth.
