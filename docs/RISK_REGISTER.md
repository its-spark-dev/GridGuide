## Blocking versus bounded limitations

The missing probability labels and partial full-network topology are **bounded limitations**, because the challenge permits risk bands, anonymized locations and simulated operational conditions. They do not block the audited core demo. Labor-hour semantics remain a material credibility issue: disclose the provisional conversion, seek organizer clarification, and do not claim validated real-world timing. The real-data CLI now passes the engine gate with 36 tests and manual access-state runs; overall status remains **YELLOW** because timing is provisional and risk uncalibrated. This supports building a clearly labeled scenario experience. It does not establish operational predictive accuracy or completion of the UI/AI challenge journey.
